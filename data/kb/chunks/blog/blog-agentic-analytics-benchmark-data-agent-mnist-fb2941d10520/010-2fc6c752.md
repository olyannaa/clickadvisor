It is interesting to observe that Claude Fable 5\.1 and Claude Opus 4\.8 reach their ceiling at turns 11 and 10\. Claude Fable 5\.1 sits at 75\.1 of its eventual 76\.6 by turn 10, and Claude Opus 4\.8 at 71\.4 of 71\.9\. Claude Fable 5 saturates only at 18, so turn efficiency varies inside a single family and is not a property of the provider. An interesting case is DeepSeek V4 Pro which scores 55\.7 at 10 turns and 74\.6 by 43, passing Opus 4\.8 at budget 36\. The extreme is Gemini 3\.8 Flash, the newest model here, at 9\.5 by 10 turns and 67\.2 by 60\. That 57\.7\-point gain is the largest on the board, and it buys it with the slowest answers here at 105 seconds per question. Gemini 3\.5 Flash goes from 6\.5 to 52\.5 and only reaches that at 59, so this run does not establish its ceiling. This demonstrates that different models offer different turn budget, token cost and pass rate profiles.
