GROUP BY. More concretely, for the house price dataset, suppose we wish to find the year in which the most expensive house was sold for each district in London. In MySQL syntax, this might be written as follows:

```
1SELECT
2	uk.district,
3	ukp.date AS most_expensive_year
4FROM uk_price_paid AS ukp
5INNER JOIN
6(
7	SELECT
8    	district,
9    	MAX(price) AS max_price
10	FROM uk_price_paid
11	WHERE town = 'LONDON'
12	GROUP BY district
13) AS uk ON (ukp.district = uk.district) AND (ukp.price = uk.max_price)
14WHERE town = 'LONDON'
15ORDER BY uk.district ASC
16LIMIT 10

In ClickHouse, the `argMax` column simplifies this significantly avoiding our INNER JOIN:

```
1SELECT
2	district,
3	CAST(argMax(date, price), 'Date') AS most_expensive_year
4FROM uk_price_paid
5WHERE town = 'LONDON'
6GROUP BY district
7ORDER BY district ASC
8LIMIT 10
