**2\.** Edit the queue trust policy to allow your bucket (`<bucket-arn>`) in your AWS account (`<aws-account-id>`) to send messages to the SQS queue (`<queue-arn>`).

```
1{
2    "Version":"2012-10-17",                   
3    "Id": "example-ID",
4    "Statement": [
5        {
6            "Sid": "AllowS3ToSendMessage",
7            "Effect": "Allow",
8            "Principal": {
9                "Service": "s3.amazonaws.com"
10            },
11            "Action": [
12                "SQS:SendMessage"
13            ],
14            "Resource": "<sqs-queue-arn>",
15            "Condition": {
16                "ArnLike": {
17                    "aws:SourceArn": "<bucket-arn>"
18                },
19                "StringEquals": {
20                    "aws:SourceAccount": "<aws-account-id>"
21                }
22            }
23        }
24    ]
```

**3\.** In the ClickHouse Cloud console, navigate to **Settings \> Network security information** and copy the IAM role ARN for your service.

![image2.png](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fimage2_2accfed654.png&w=2048&q=75)

**4\.** Back in the AWS Console, go to **IAM \> Roles \> Create role**. Choose **Custom trust policy** and paste the IAM role ARN for your ClickHouse Cloud service.

```
1{
2  "Version": "2012-10-17",
3  "Statement": [
4    {
5      "Sid": "AllowAssumeRole",
6      "Effect": "Allow",
7      "Principal": {
8        "AWS": "<ch-cloud-arn>"
9      },
10      "Action": "sts:AssumeRole"
11    }
12  ]
13}
```

**5\.** Create an inline policy for the IAM role with the required permissions to scan and fetch objects from the S3 bucket and manage messages in the SQS queue.

```
1{
2  "Version": "2012-10-17",
3  "Statement": [
4    {
5      "Sid": "S3BucketMetadataAccess",
6      "Effect": "Allow",
7      "Action": [
8        "s3:GetBucketLocation",
9        "s3:ListBucket"
10      ],
11      "Resource": "<bucket-arn>"
12    },
13    {
14      "Sid": "AllowGetListObjects",
15      "Effect": "Allow",
16      "Action": [
17        "s3:Get*",
18        "s3:List*"
19      ],
20      "Resource": "<bucket-arn>/*"
21    },
22    {
23      "Sid": "SQSNotificationsAccess",
24      "Effect": "Allow",
25      "Action": [
26        "sqs:DeleteMessage",
27        "sqs:ListQueues",
28        "sqs:ReceiveMessage",
29        "sqs:GetQueueAttributes"
30      ],
31    "Resource": "<sqs-queue-arn>"
32    }
33  ]
34}
```
