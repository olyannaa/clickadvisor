We expect this Nullable overhead to be minimal, however, due to the data volumes being low for ClickHouse.

We can insert data from Parquet files into this schema, using the gcs function and the following `INSERT INTO SELECT`. This statement is the same for both tables.

```
1INSERT INTO ga_daily SELECT
2  event_date::Date as event_date,
3  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(event_timestamp) as event_timestamp,
4  ifNull(event_name, '') as event_name,
5  mapFromArrays(
6	arrayMap(x -> x.1::String, event_params),
7	arrayMap(x -> arrayFilter(val -> isNotNull(val),array(untuple(x.2:: Tuple(string_value Nullable(String), int_value Nullable(String), float_value Nullable(String), double_value Nullable(String))))::Array(Nullable(String)))[1], event_params)
8  )::Map(String, String) as event_params,
9  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(ifNull(event_previous_timestamp,0)) as event_previous_timestamp,
10  event_bundle_sequence_id,
11  event_server_timestamp_offset,
12  user_id,
13  user_pseudo_id,
14  privacy_info,
15  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(ifNull(user_first_touch_timestamp,0)) as user_first_touch_timestamp,
16  device,
17  geo,
18  app_info,
19  traffic_source,
20  stream_id,
21  platform,
22  event_dimensions,
23  collected_traffic_source,
24  is_active_user
25FROM  gcs('https://storage.googleapis.com/clickhouse-website/ga-*.parquet','<key>','<secret>')
```

Most of the columns here are a straight mapping. We do, however, convert the `event_params` column to a `Map(String,String)` from the format to `Array(Tuple(key Nullable(String), value Tuple(string_value Nullable(String), int_value Nullable(Int64), float_value Nullable(Float64), double_value Nullable(Float64))))` to make querying simpler syntactically.

### GCS to ClickHouse \#

While our internal data warehouse [has its own custom mechanisms](https://clickhouse.com/blog/building-a-data-warehouse-with-clickhouse) for loading data, ClickHouse users can reproduce the above architecture with either a scheduled `INSERT INTO SELECT` e.g. [using simple cron](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-bigquery-migrating-data-for-realtime-queries#scheduling-data-import) or via [Cloud Scheduler](https://cloud.google.com/scheduler), or using the recently [released S3Queue table engine](https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/engines/table-engines/integrations/s3queue). We demonstrate this below:

```
1CREATE TABLE ga_queue
2ENGINE = S3Queue('https://storage.googleapis.com/clickhouse-website/daily/*.parquet', '<key>', '<secret>', 'Parquet')
3SETTINGS mode = 'unordered', s3queue_enable_logging_to_s3queue_log=1, s3queue_tracked_files_limit=10000
4
5CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW ga_mv TO ga_daily AS
6SELECT
7  event_date::Date as event_date,
8  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(event_timestamp) as event_timestamp,
9  ifNull(event_name, '') as event_name,
10  mapFromArrays(
11	arrayMap(x -> x.1::String, event_params),
12	arrayMap(x -> arrayFilter(val -> isNotNull(val),array(untuple(x.2:: Tuple(string_value Nullable(String), int_value Nullable(String), float_value Nullable(String), double_value Nullable(String))))::Array(Nullable(String)))[1], event_params)
13  )::Map(String, String) as event_params,
14  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(ifNull(event_previous_timestamp,0)) as event_previous_timestamp,
15  event_bundle_sequence_id,
16  event_server_timestamp_offset,
17  user_id,
18  user_pseudo_id,
19  privacy_info,
20  fromUnixTimestamp64Micro(ifNull(user_first_touch_timestamp,0)) as user_first_touch_timestamp,
21  device,
22  geo,
23  app_info,
24  traffic_source,
25  stream_id,
26  platform,
27  event_dimensions,
28  collected_traffic_source,
29  is_active_user
30FROM ga_queue
```
