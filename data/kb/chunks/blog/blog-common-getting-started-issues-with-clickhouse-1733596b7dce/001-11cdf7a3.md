# Getting started with ClickHouse? 13 mistakes and how to avoid them

![Dale McDirmid](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FDale_Mc_Dirmid_8016f87452.png&w=96&q=75)![tom schreiber headshot](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Ftom_schreiber_headshot_a0cb0ce627.jpeg&w=96&q=75)![1597577736383](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2F1597577736383_ed4e6f28a2.jpeg&w=96&q=75)[Dale McDiarmid](/authors/dale-mcdiarmid), [Tom Schreiber](/authors/tom-schreiber) and [Geoff Genz](/authors/geoff-genz)Feb 20, 2026 · 36 minutes readAt ClickHouse, we are constantly thinking about our getting started experience. ClickHouse is a complex and powerful piece of software that introduces new concepts for many, which can lead to mistakes. In this post, we highlight the most common 13 mistakes we see our new users encounter, why they occur and the correct approach.

For users encountering challenges managing ClickHouse at scale, [ClickHouse Cloud](https://clickhouse.cloud/signUp) automatically handles many of the common getting\-started and subsequent scaling challenges.

## 1\. Too many parts \#

An often\-seen ClickHouse error:

```
1DB::Exception: Too many parts
```
Copy command
This error will often be experienced when inserting data and will be present in ClickHouse logs or in a response to an INSERT request. To understand this error, users need to understand the concept of a part in ClickHouse.
