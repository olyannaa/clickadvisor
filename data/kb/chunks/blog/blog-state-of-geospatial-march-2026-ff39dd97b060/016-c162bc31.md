We end up with the following query, which also colors the point dark so that it's easier to see:

```
1WITH
2    readWKT('POLYGON((-0.51 51.63,-0.10 51.69,0.26 51.65,0.33 51.52,0.25 51.30,-0.06 51.28,-0.33 51.32,-0.51 51.45,-0.51 51.63))') AS london,
3    (-0.0759, 51.5081) AS tower_of_london,
4    replaceOne(svg(london), 'style=""', 'style="fill:#FAFF69;stroke:#1A1A2E;stroke-width:0.005"') AS london_svg,
5    replaceOne(replaceOne(svg(tower_of_london), 'r="5"', 'r="0.02"'), 'style=""', 'style="fill:#161517;"') AS tower_svg
6SELECT concat(
7    '<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="-0.55 51.24 0.92 0.49" width="600" height="300">',
8    '<g transform="scale(1,-1) translate(0,-102.97)">',
9    london_svg,
10    tower_svg,
11    '</g></svg>');
```
