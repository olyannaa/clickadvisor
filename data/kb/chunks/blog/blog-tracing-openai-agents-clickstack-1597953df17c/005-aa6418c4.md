So far so good. We have a trace right at the beginning, followed by a series of spans. Those spans represent:

1. A listing of all the MCP tools
2. A call to the `run_select_query` function
3. A response returned by the LLM.

Currently, we aren’t capturing the model name in the response span, which is an important piece of information. That data does exist on the `span_data` object, but isn’t exported. Let’s update our exporter to capture the model:

```
1class ClickHouseExporter(TracingExporter):
2    def export(self, items: list) -> None:
3        for item in items:
4            if "Span" in type(item).__name__:                
5                span_data = {}
6                if hasattr(item, "span_data") and item.span_data:
7                    if hasattr(item.span_data, "export"):
8                        span_data = item.span_data.export()
9                    
10                    if hasattr(item.span_data, "response") and item.span_data.response:
11                        if hasattr(item.span_data.response, "model"):
12                            span_data["model"] = str(item.span_data.response.model)
13                
14                span_export = {**item.export(), "span_data": span_data}
15                print(span_export)
16            else:
17                print(item.export())
```
