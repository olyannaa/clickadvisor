# SET allow\_preview\_features \= 1: Self\-service previews in ClickHouse Cloud

![t02em6f031p u0a0ndka733 20c6d25c254b 512](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FT02_EM_6_F031_P_U0_A0_NDKA_733_20c6d25c254b_512_4fefb1f6af.png&w=96&q=75)[Amy Chen](/authors/amy-chen)Nov 13, 2026 · 6 minutes readIf you've been around the ClickHouse community for a while, you know that we ship fast. But all that speed comes with a side effect. It can be easy to miss things. And often the newest stuff is the easiest to miss. Historically, our preview Cloud features have lived behind a velvet rope, and to get in, you had to know a guy. Maybe your account team mentioned it on a call. Maybe you said the magic words in a support ticket. Whatever it may be, it caused unnecessary friction. And as a product manager, user feedback is priceless, so the more we can get, the better.

We also know that you, as a ClickHouse user, want to be the most innovative AND want access to all the toys. So today, we are democratizing access.

We're rolling out **Self\-Service Previews** in ClickHouse Cloud: one page in the console where *everyone* can see what's in preview. This will be your place to see what previews are available and ready for either self\-service enablement or need a waitlist signup. No guy required.

## Previews \#

Longtime ClickHouse users know that we have these magical settings that start with `allow_experimental_*` that give you access to new features that are usable but might need some more finesse.

Previews are the same idea for ClickHouse Cloud: features that are built and ready for feedback but not quite ready to be enabled by default.
