With a size of 1\.4 billion records and 32 columns, it is used in many aggregation queries that power the OONI [Measurement Aggregation Toolkit (MAT)](https://explorer.ooni.org/chart/mat) and their internal data analysis tools. The aggregation queries run on the dataset are built based on user configurable parameters and at a high level have the following structure:

```
1SELECT 
2countIf(anomaly = 't' AND confirmed = 'f' AND msm_failure = 'f') AS anomaly_count,
3countIf(confirmed = 't' AND msm_failure = 'f') AS confirmed_count,
4countIf(msm_failure = 't') AS failure_count,
5countIf(anomaly = 'f' AND confirmed = 'f' AND msm_failure = 'f') AS ok_count,
6COUNT(*) AS measurement_count,
7[user configured grouping parameter_a],
8[user configured grouping parameter_b]
9WHERE
10[user configured filter]
11GROUP BY [user configured grouping parameter_a], [user configured grouping parameter_b]
```
