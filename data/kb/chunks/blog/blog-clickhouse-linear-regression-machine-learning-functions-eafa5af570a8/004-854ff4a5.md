## Model evaluation \#

We can now use the fitted model to make predictions for the remaining 20% of our dataset. We will do this by comparing the predicted delivery times with the actuals to calculate the accuracy of our model.

```
1CREATE VIEW deliveries_results AS WITH
2    (SELECT avg(delivery_distance) FROM deliveries_dataset WHERE training = 1) AS loc,
3    (SELECT stddevSamp(delivery_distance) FROM deliveries_dataset WHERE training = 1) AS scale,
4    (SELECT state from deliveries_model) AS model
5SELECT
6    toInt32(delivery_time) as ACTUAL,
7    toInt32(exp(evalMLMethod(
8        model, 
9        assumeNotNull((delivery_distance - loc) / scale),
10        Hour7,
11        Hour8,
12        Hour9,
13        Hour10,
14        Hour11,
15        Hour12,
16        Hour13,
17        Hour14,
18        Hour15,
19        Hour16
20    ))) AS PREDICTED
21FROM deliveries_dataset  WHERE training = 0
```
