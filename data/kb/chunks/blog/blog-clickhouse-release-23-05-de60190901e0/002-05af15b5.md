Note below how we are required to specify a connection string, container and blob path to align with [Azure Blob storage concepts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/storage-blobs-introduction#blob-storage-resources). In the examples below we query the [UK price paid dataset](https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/getting-started/example-datasets/uk-price-paid).

6GROUP BY year
7ORDER BY year ASC
