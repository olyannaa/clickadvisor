```
1SELECT sum(rows) AS docs,
2       formatReadableSize(sum(data_uncompressed_bytes))              AS col_uncompressed,
3       formatReadableSize(sum(data_compressed_bytes))                AS col_compressed,
4       formatReadableSize(sum(secondary_indices_uncompressed_bytes)) AS idx_uncompressed,
5       formatReadableSize(sum(secondary_indices_compressed_bytes))   AS idx_compressed,
6       formatReadableSize(sum(bytes_on_disk))                        AS on_disk
7FROM system.parts
8WHERE database = 'vectors' AND table = 'documents' AND active
```

Copy commandEmbeddings really are close to incompressible \- 178\.36 KiB down to 162\.34 KiB is a compression ratio of **1\.1x**, where log and metric columns often manage 10x or more, because high\-entropy floats give the codecs nothing to work with. (That figure covers all three columns, but `embedding` is most of it; `content` is a few KiB of the total.) And the HNSW index is **29\.8% of the part** at this scale, 69 KiB of the 232 KiB total, buying you nothing over a brute\-force scan of 57 rows. So if you’re asking "should I add the index?" for a small corpus: not yet.
