## Why I use ClickHouse for my web analytics tool \#

![](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Foct2026_nl_image3_5408048ab7.png&w=2048&q=75)

Philip explains why he chose ClickHouse for Lienks, his privacy\-friendly web analytics tool. PostgreSQL stores application data, such as users, workspaces, and billing, while pageviews and custom events are stored in ClickHouse.

Philip points out that PostgreSQL would handle Lienks' current size, but chose ClickHouse up front because he hopes to scale to billions of events and wouldn’t want to migrate if that happens.

## Why Databricks can't match ClickHouse Cloud for real\-time analytics \#

![](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Foct2026_nl_image4_fc87049c80.jpg&w=2048&q=75)

Last month, Tom Schreiber and Lionel Palacin introduced [CostBench](https://clickhouse.com/blog/costbench-real-time-performance-per-dollar?utm_source=clickhouse&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=202610-newsletter&ref=newsletter), a benchmark that measures the end\-to\-end performance and cost of real\-time analytics across cloud data warehouses. They went on to show that [Snowflake can’t match ClickHouse Cloud for real\-time analytics](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-vs-snowflake-real-time-performance-per-dollar?utm_source=clickhouse&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=202610-newsletter&ref=newsletter).

This month, they put Databricks through its paces, ingesting 113\.2 billion stock\-market quotes while queries run. ClickHouse Cloud delivered 752× better end\-to\-end performance per dollar.

ClickHouse uses incremental materialized views to keep its pre\-aggregations current at insert time, whereas Databricks splits that work across ingestion, asynchronous clustering, and materialized view refreshes. As a result, Databricks' preparation cost came to $695\.60, compared to $28\.69 for ClickHouse.

➡️ [Read the blog post](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-vs-databricks-real-time-performance-per-dollar?utm_source=clickhouse&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=202610-newsletter&ref=newsletter)

## Why BigQuery can't match ClickHouse Cloud for real\-time analytics \#

![](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Foct2026_nl_image5_cfbec2e833.jpg&w=2048&q=75)

Tom and Lionel also ran CostBench against BigQuery, ingesting 113\.2 billion quotes while queries ran.

ClickHouse Cloud performs 438× better per dollar under BigQuery's Capacity pricing and 512× better under On\-demand pricing.

ClickHouse sorts and pre\-aggregates data on insert, while BigQuery leaves catch\-up work for query time. The total query runtime was 58\.29 seconds for ClickHouse and 49\.36 minutes for BigQuery.

➡️ [Read the blog post](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-vs-bigquery-real-time-performance-per-dollar?utm_source=clickhouse&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=202610-newsletter&ref=newsletter)
