2   5 AS k_val,
3   (
4       SELECT max(iteration)

11       (
12           SELECT
13               centroid,
14               avgForEachMerge(vector) AS position
15           FROM centroids
16           WHERE (iteration = c_iteration) AND (k = k_val)

18       )
19   ) AS c_centroids
20SELECT
21   (arraySort(c -> (c.2), arrayMap(x -> (x.1, L2Distance(x.2, vector)), c_centroids))[1]).1 AS cluster,
22   floor(avg(pickup_day_of_month)) AS pickup_day_of_month,
23   round(avg(pickup_hour)) AS avg_pickup_hour,
24   round(avg(fare_amount)) AS avg_fare_amount,
25   round(avg(total_amount)) AS avg_total_amount

## Scaling \#

Our previous example uses only a 3m row subset of the NYC taxi rides. Testing on a larger dataset for all of taxi rides for 2009 (170m rows), we can complete clustering for k\=5 in around 3 mins with a ClickHouse service using 60 cores.
