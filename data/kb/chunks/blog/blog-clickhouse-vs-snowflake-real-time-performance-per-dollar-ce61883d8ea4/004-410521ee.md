**① PREPARATION COST**

 The complete 113\.2\-billion\-row ingest includes all metered ingestion and preparation work. ClickHouse’s cost includes both 2\-CPU nodes in its smallest calibrated high\-availability ingest service. Snowflake’s separately metered Snowpipe Streaming and serverless MV refresh are both included.
 

**② QUERY COST AND ③ RUNTIME**

 Query cost prices each accepted query’s full client\-visible duration at the applicable read\-side rate. Runtime sums those durations over the same active\-ingestion workload, aligned by row count. These normalized query costs are separate from complete warehouse invoices. The query\-comparison and pricing notes below give the accepted counts, rates, and exclusions.
 

**TARGET VERSUS OBSERVED RATE**

 The target implies about 28 hours for 100 billion rows. Observed throughput averaged approximately 0\.86 million rows/s for ClickHouse and 0\.90 million for Snowflake. They reached 100 billion rows after roughly 32 and 31 hours, and completed 113\.2 billion rows after roughly 37 and 35 hours, respectively. Client pacing, parallel streams, and batching targeted an exact rate but could not guarantee one.
 

**REPORTING WINDOW**

 Per\-query latency charts stop at 100 billion rows. Preparation costs cover the complete ingest; accumulated query runtime and cost use the full accepted active\-ingestion observations aligned by row count. Displayed totals are rounded.
 

ClickHouse Cloud led in all three measured components:

**① Preparation cost:** $28\.69 versus $79\.42\.  

**② Normalized query cost:** about $0\.05 versus $15\.99\.  

**③ Accumulated query runtime:** 1\.03 minutes versus 2\.13 hours.
