Snowflake uses the same two query paths, with additional work when its summaries lag.

## How Snowflake serves queries \#

The diagram below shows Snowflake’s Small [Interactive Warehouse](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/interactive) serving both workloads, with an estimated 16 CPUs and [approximately 600 GB](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/interactive#choosing-a-size-for-an-interactive-warehouse) of [local SSD](https://medium.com/snowflake/cost-effective-performance-with-snowflake-interactive-warehouses-3892ede05f4c) cache.

**Drill\-downs:** Queries prune ordered raw data in the [Interactive Table](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/254400222ce969083b67c61e51cab05a2d707df1/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/t2/setup_streaming.sql#L27) directly. Its (sym, t) clustering, applied during ingestion, lets them skip unrelated quotes when filtering by symbol.

**Interactive aggregations:** Queries target the [Interactive MV](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/254400222ce969083b67c61e51cab05a2d707df1/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/t2/setup_streaming.sql#L71) and catch up on read by combining its summaries with rows added to the [Interactive Table](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/254400222ce969083b67c61e51cab05a2d707df1/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/t2/setup_streaming.sql#L27) since the last refresh. The answer therefore includes current data.

**Timeout and fallback:** Interactive Warehouses [cancel queries that exceed five seconds](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/interactive#automatically-handling-statement-timeouts); the limit can only be lowered. We configured a separate Gen2 Small standard warehouse, also estimated at 16 CPUs, to receive eligible timed\-out queries through Snowflake’s [automatic fallback mechanism](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/interactive#automatically-handling-statement-timeouts).

HOW ARE SNOWFLAKE’S CATCH\-UP AND FALLBACK PATHS INTERPRETED? (click to expand)

**CURRENT\-ANSWER CONTRACT**

Queries against an Interactive MV include the rows added to the base table since its last refresh. The amount of newer data grows between refreshes and shrinks when a refresh catches up, explaining the rising\-and\-reset pattern in the aggregate curves.

**FALLBACK CONFIGURATION**

The tested Interactive Warehouse used a five\-second limit and a separate Gen2 Small fallback warehouse for eligible queries. The benchmark’s normalized\-cost model uses elapsed time as a pricing proxy; it does not establish which warehouse physically executed each query.

**ATTRIBUTION RULE**

Each accepted query with a full client\-visible duration strictly greater than five seconds is priced for its entire duration at the Gen2 Small rate. Queries at or below five seconds use the Interactive Small rate. Exactly five seconds remains Interactive\-priced. Each query belongs to one bucket, without an additional Interactive charge for fallback\-priced work.
