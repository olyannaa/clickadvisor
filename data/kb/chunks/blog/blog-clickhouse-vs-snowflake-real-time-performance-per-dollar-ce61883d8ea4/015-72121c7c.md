> ClickHouse Cloud delivered 412× better end\-to\-end performance per dollar while ingesting fresh data and serving current answers.

The biggest serving advantage came from interactive aggregations. ClickHouse read current summaries; Snowflake had to combine its summaries with newer rows to return current answers. Preparing that work during ingestion paid off in both query speed and cost.

That is why Snowflake can’t match ClickHouse Cloud for real\-time analytics.
