### Accumulated query cost and runtime \#

Those per\-query differences accumulate: interactive aggregations account for most of the gap. The charts below combine both workloads and price their measured runtimes using the benchmark’s read\-side cost model.

ClickHouse Cloud accumulated 1\.03 minutes of query runtime and Snowflake 2\.13 hours. Their normalized query\-serving costs were about $0\.05 and $15\.99, respectively. These are normalized costs for the query work, not complete warehouse invoices.

> Across the query workload, ClickHouse Cloud was 124× faster overall, with 298× lower normalized query cost.

## From the field: Appcues \#

Appcues’ migration from Snowflake to ClickHouse Cloud shows the same full\-path efficiency in production: faster queries, fresher data, and lower analytics spend for its customer\-facing dashboards. “Page load time has to be practically instant,” says Chris Brookins, VP of Engineering. Its Snowflake\-and\-Airflow stack rolled up data every five to ten minutes, delaying fresh results and adding infrastructure to maintain. Computing aggregates during inserts let the team retire its Airflow infrastructure.

P95 query latency fell from over 20 seconds to **under 2 seconds**, and newly arrived events became queryable in **about 5 seconds**, down from ten minutes or more. Analytics spend fell **23%**, even as Appcues added workloads and features. [Read Appcues’ migration story](https://clickhouse.com/blog/appcues-customer-engagement-analytics).

## Snowflake can’t match ClickHouse Cloud for real\-time analytics \#

While fresh data kept arriving, ClickHouse Cloud outperformed Snowflake across the complete workload:

**① Preparation cost: 2\.8× lower** for ingestion, ordering, and pre\-aggregation.  

**② Normalized query cost: 298× lower** for the matched queries.  

**③ Accumulated query runtime: 124× lower** across both query workloads.
