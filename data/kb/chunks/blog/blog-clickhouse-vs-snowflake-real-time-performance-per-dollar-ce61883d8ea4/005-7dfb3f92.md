PRICING, FORMULAS, AND THE 412× SCORE (click to expand) **PRICING BASIS**

The tested Enterprise pricing in AWS us\-east was $3 per Snowflake credit and $0\.3903 per ClickHouse Cloud compute unit (CU) per hour. Snowflake’s serverless charges use its metering history. Displayed values below are rounded; the [accepted summary](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/results/t2/charts/run14/full_path_cost_performance_clickhouse_vs_snowflake_wide_summary.json) uses full\-precision inputs.

**① COMPLETE PREPARATION COST**

This covers ingestion and preparation of all 113\.2 billion rows.

**ClickHouse Cloud:** [2 CUs](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/clickhouse-cloud/costs/out_t2/ingest.json) × 36\.75 hours × $0\.3903/CU\-hour \= $28\.68705\. This includes both nodes of the HA ingest service.

**Snowflake ingestion:** [Snowpipe Streaming processed 4\.6762 TiB](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/costs/out/t2/run14/snowpipe_streaming.json), metered at 17\.71783 credits under the [0\.0037\-credits\-per\-uncompressed\-GB rate](https://www.snowflake.com/legal-files/CreditConsumptionTable.pdf). Multiplying the unrounded credits by $3 gives $53\.15348\. Ingest\-time clustering is included; no ingest warehouse was used.

**Snowflake refresh:** [8\.75553 metered credits](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/costs/out/t2/run14/mv_refresh.json) × $3/credit \= $26\.26659\. Total preparation cost: $53\.15348 \+ $26\.26659 \= $79\.42007\.

**② NORMALIZED QUERY COST**

Each accepted query is priced as its full client\-visible duration × the applicable per\-second read\-side rate.

**ClickHouse Cloud:** 8 CUs × $0\.3903/CU\-hour × ([11\.361s aggregate](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/clickhouse-cloud/costs/out_t2/matched/snowflake_run14/dashboard.json) \+ [50\.466s drill\-down](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/clickhouse-cloud/costs/out_t2/matched/snowflake_run14/drilldown.json)) ÷ 3,600 \= $0\.05362\.

**Snowflake Interactive\-priced work:** ([946\.509s aggregate](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/costs/out/t2/run14/dashboard.json) \+ [74\.815s drill\-down](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/costs/out/t2/run14/drilldown.json)) × 1\.2 credits/hour × $3/credit ÷ 3,600 \= $1\.02132\.

**Snowflake fallback\-priced work:** [6,653\.660s aggregate](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/snowflake/costs/out/t2/run14/dashboard.json) × 2\.7 credits/hour × $3/credit ÷ 3,600 \= $14\.97074\. Total normalized query cost: $1\.02132 \+ $14\.97074 \= $15\.99207\.

**Fallback boundary:** The elapsed\-time proxy assigns jobs above five seconds to Gen2 Small for their full duration and all others to Interactive Small. It excludes standing fallback capacity, idle capacity, minimum billing, and an additional Interactive charge for fallback\-priced jobs. The allocation is a cost model, not a reconstruction of which warehouse physically executed each job.

**③ ACCUMULATED QUERY RUNTIME**

**ClickHouse Cloud:** 11\.361s aggregate \+ 50\.466s drill\-down \= 61\.827s.

**Snowflake:** 7,600\.169s aggregate \+ 74\.815s drill\-down \= 7,674\.984s.

**COMPARISON BOUNDARY**

① covers the complete ingest. ② and ③ use the accepted active\-ingestion sets aligned by row count: 209 × 4 aggregate queries and 35 × 2 drill\-downs per system. Later post\-ingestion observations and database storage are excluded. Query cost is normalized to accepted query work; it is not the complete warehouse invoice.

**FINAL SCORE**

**ClickHouse Cloud:** ($28\.68705 \+ $0\.05362\) × 61\.827 \= 1,776\.9494\.
