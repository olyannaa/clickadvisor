## How Redshift Serverless serves queries \#

The diagram below shows a separate **32\-RPU consumer workgroup** reading shared data from the writer. The [live datashare](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/redshift/latest/dg/datashare-overview.html) exposes the streaming view and daily aggregate MV without copying them into a second serving database.

**Drill\-downs:** The [drill\-down queries](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/sql/queries_drilldown_super.sql) filter the typed sym column in quotes\_streamed, then navigate and cast measure fields from the payload. Its (sym, t) sort key supports pruning, but incoming rows can be read before background sorting completes.

**Interactive aggregations:** Queries read the [daily aggregate MV](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/sql/queries_dashboard.sql) as of its last completed refresh. They do not merge newer streaming\-view rows into the answer at query time, so the returned summary can trail incoming data.

The diagram’s two paths show where preparation remains visible to readers: drill\-downs can encounter unsorted data, while aggregate queries can read an earlier summary snapshot.

What do Redshift’s refresh and datashare guarantees mean? (click to expand)

**Snapshot contract:**

A query reads committed contents of the daily MV. The benchmark did not force a refresh before every query or reconstruct its answer from newer streaming rows. The measured aggregate latency therefore retains Redshift’s summary\-refresh lag.

**Refresh contract:**

AUTO REFRESH YES drives streaming ingestion. A controller separately refreshes the downstream MVs because Redshift does not permit [automatic refresh](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/redshift/latest/dg/materialized-view-create-sql-command.html#mv_CREATE_MATERIALIZED_VIEW-limitations) on an MV defined on another MV. RESTRICT updates only the named child from the committed parent state. This avoids stacking the typed projection’s refresh delay into daily\-summary freshness.

**Live\-share contract:**

Sharing exposes the writer’s committed objects to the reader workgroup. It separates read compute from preparation compute; it does not make those objects fresher or sort their newest rows before queries access them.

**Read\-path boundary:**

The headline drill\-down results come from the live streaming view. A typed projection was also maintained and measured as an alternative. Its refresh and storage\-layout work are retained in the shared preparation scenario, even though its query executions are excluded from this article’s score.
