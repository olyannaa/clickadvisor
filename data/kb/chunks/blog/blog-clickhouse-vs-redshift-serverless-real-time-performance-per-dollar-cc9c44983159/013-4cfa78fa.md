Why use Redshift’s streaming view, and what about typed columns? (click to expand)

**Streaming view:**

The [streaming view](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/sql/setup_streaming.sql) keeps the payload as SUPER and promotes sym and t to typed sort\-key columns. D1 and D2 filter the typed sym column, preserving pruning intent; they cast the payload’s measure fields when computing results. This is a different representation from ClickHouse’s typed raw table.

**Typed alternative:**

A sibling typed MV projects the payload fields into columns, with the same (sym, t) sort key and EVEN distribution. Its queries implement the same Redshift drill\-down logic. Both representations were measured during the long run.
