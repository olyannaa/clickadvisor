Pricing, formulas, and the 1,996× score (click to expand)

**Pricing basis:**

ClickHouse Cloud uses **$0\.3903 per compute unit (CU) per hour**. Redshift uses the checked\-in [**eu\-west\-2 rate**](https://aws.amazon.com/redshift/pricing/) **of $0\.467/RPU\-hour**. [MSK](https://aws.amazon.com/msk/pricing/) uses **$0\.47175 per broker\-hour** and **$0\.116 per GB\-month** of broker storage. The [accepted full\-path summary](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/costs/out/t2/full_path_super.json) retains the model and full\-precision inputs.

**① Complete preparation cost:**

The full\-path scenario includes writer compute over the complete 113,227\-second producer window, MSK broker uptime, and prorated broker storage. The writer covers streaming ingestion, background sorting, and both child\-MV refreshes.

**ClickHouse Cloud:**

2 CUs × 36\.75 hours × $0\.3903/CU\-hour \= **$28\.68705**, including both HA ingest nodes.

**Redshift writer:**

The [32\-active\-RPU scenario](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/costs/writer_capacity_assumption.json) uses **32 × (113,227 ÷ 3,600\) hours × $0\.467/RPU\-hour \= $470\.01785778**. The long run used 128 writer RPUs; the 32\-RPU cost scenario is grounded in subsequent keep\-up characterization and is not original\-run billing.

**MSK:**

Three brokers × producer uptime × $0\.47175/broker\-hour, plus 1,500 GB × $0\.116/GB\-month × uptime\-hours ÷ 730, totals **$52\.00912922**.

**Shared preparation path:**

Writer \+ MSK \= **$522\.02698699**. That complete total is charged once in this score. It is neither split between the two read alternatives nor doubled within the comparison.

**② Normalized query cost:**

ClickHouse prices recorded durations. Redshift allocates each hour’s reader [SYS\_SERVERLESS\_USAGE.compute\_seconds](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/redshift/latest/dg/SYS_SERVERLESS_USAGE.html#SYS_SERVERLESS_USAGE-table-columns) across statements that began in that hour, in proportion to statement elapsed\_time; the accepted query share is priced at $0\.467/RPU\-hour.

**ClickHouse Cloud:**

8 CUs × $0\.3903/CU\-hour × (10\.207s aggregate \+ 47\.915s drill\-down) ÷ 3,600 ≈ **$0\.05041**, using the rounded cost\-summary inputs.

**Redshift aggregate work:**

The [committed hourly allocations](https://github.com/ClickHouse/CostBench/blob/main/full-path-realtime/quotes/redshift-serverless/results/t2/billed_per_query_reader.csv) assign **$2\.955693203** to the 760 aggregate executions. This uses recorded reader compute and statement duration, rather than a flat 32\-RPU rate multiplied by each query’s wall time.

**Redshift drill\-down work:**

The 66 executions receive **$22\.011782831**, giving total normalized query cost of **$24\.967476034**. Together, the two workloads receive **53\.4635461 RPU\-hours** of allocated reader compute.
