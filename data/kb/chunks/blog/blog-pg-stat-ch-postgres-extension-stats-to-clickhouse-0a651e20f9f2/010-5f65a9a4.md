With raw events per query in ClickHouse, you can ask questions that are impossible with aggregate\-only systems. Here are some real queries:

**Find the individual queries causing cache misses:**

```
1SELECT
2    query_id,
3    any(query) AS sample_query,
4    count() AS executions,
5    avg(shared_blks_read) AS avg_physical_reads,
6    sum(shared_blks_read) AS total_physical_reads
7FROM events_raw
8WHERE ts_start > now() - INTERVAL 24 HOUR
9  AND shared_blks_read > 1000
10GROUP BY query_id
11ORDER BY total_physical_reads DESC
12LIMIT 20;
```

**Showing the most expensive queries with percentile distributions:**

```
1SELECT
2    query_id,
3    any(query) AS sample_query,
4    count() AS executions,
5    round(100 * sum(duration_us) / (
6        SELECT sum(duration_us)
7        FROM pg_stat_ch.events_raw
8        WHERE ts_start > now() - INTERVAL 24 HOUR
9    ), 2) AS pct_runtime,
10    count() AS cnt,
    round(sum(duration_us) / 1e6, 2) AS total_seconds,
11    round(quantile(0.50)(duration_us) / 1000, 0) AS p50_ms,
12    round(quantile(0.90)(duration_us) / 1000, 0) AS p90_ms,
13    round(quantile(0.99)(duration_us) / 1000, 0) AS p99_ms
14FROM pg_stat_ch.events_raw
15WHERE ts_start > now() - INTERVAL 24 HOUR
16GROUP BY query_id
17ORDER BY sum(duration_us) DESC
18LIMIT 20;
```
