This highlights some obvious times of the year e.g. 4th July, when commuter traffic is significantly lower.

```


SELECT
    toStartOfWeek(transit_timestamp) AS week,
    'weekday' AS period,
    sum(ridership) AS total
FROM mta.transit_data
WHERE toDayOfWeek(transit_timestamp) <= 5
GROUP BY week
ORDER BY week ASC
UNION ALL
SELECT
    toStartOfWeek(transit_timestamp) AS week,
    'weekend' AS period,
    sum(ridership) AS total
FROM mta.transit_data
WHERE toDayOfWeek(transit_timestamp) > 5
GROUP BY week
ORDER BY week ASC
