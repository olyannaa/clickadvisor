Limiting our analysis to those users who have forked the repository, our query:

```
1WITH logins AS
2	(
3    	SELECT DISTINCT actor_login AS logins
4    	FROM github_events
5    	WHERE (repo_name IN ['yandex/ClickHouse', 'ClickHouse/ClickHouse']) AND (event_type = 'ForkEvent')
6	)
7SELECT
8	actor_login,
9	windowFunnel(2592000)(created_at, event_type = 'ForkEvent', event_type = 'PullRequestEvent') AS step
10FROM github_events
11WHERE (repo_name IN ['yandex/ClickHouse', 'ClickHouse/ClickHouse']) AND (actor_login IN (logins)) AND (event_type IN ['ForkEvent', 'PullRequestEvent'])
12GROUP BY actor_login
13LIMIT 10
```
