**Filtered to the local region** (`Region = 'us-east-2'`, where the query was initiated), a similar query to collect errors by component completes in **270 ms**, processing 2\.7M rows.

```
1SELECT
2    LA['component'] AS component,
3    count() AS errors
4FROM otel.generic_logs
5WHERE (EventDate = today()) AND (EventTime >= (now() - toIntervalHour(1))) AND (SeverityText = 'ERROR') AND (Namespace = 'o11y') AND (PodName LIKE 'loghouse-agent%') AND (Region IN ('us-east-1'))
6GROUP BY ALL
7ORDER BY ALL ASC
```
