Copy command**Without a `Region` filter**, this fans out to all 36 cells. The query returns rows for 29 regions with errors and completes in about **1\.6 seconds**, processing 33M rows. That's slower than a typical fleet\-wide query, and `text_log` shows exactly why: the coordinator hit five stale pooled TCP connections, and one of them was to `asia-southeast1`. A full TCP\+TLS handshake to Singapore took roughly 700 ms, after which the Singapore cell needed another \~600 ms to execute and stream back. Local planning, primary\-key pruning, and aggregation took only \~75 ms in total — the rest is exactly the tail\-latency story we wanted to avoid.
