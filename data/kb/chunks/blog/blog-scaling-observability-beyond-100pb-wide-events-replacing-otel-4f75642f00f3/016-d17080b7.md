SQL allows us to express joins, time\-based operations, and transformations that would be difficult or impossible to perform in typical log query tools. One example is identifying pod termination times by correlating Kubernetes event streams. The query below uses ASOF JOIN to align Killing and Created events for the same container, calculating the time between termination and restart:

```
1WITH
2    KE AS
3    (
4        SELECT *
5        FROM loghouse.kube_events
6        WHERE (FirstTimestamp >= '2025-03-10 01:00:00') AND (FirstTimestamp <= '2025-03-11 01:00:00') AND (Reason IN ['Killing']) AND (FieldPath LIKE 'spec.containers{c-%-server}')
7    ),
8    CE AS
9    (
10        SELECT *
11        FROM loghouse.kube_events
12        WHERE (FirstTimestamp >= '2025-03-10 01:00:00') AND (FirstTimestamp <= '2025-03-11 01:00:00') AND (Reason IN ['Created']) AND (FieldPath LIKE 'spec.containers{c-%-server}')
13    )
14SELECT
15    Name,
16    KE.FirstTimestamp AS killTime,
17    CE.FirstTimestamp AS createTime,
18    createTime - killTime AS delta,
19    formatReadableTimeDelta(createTime - killTime) AS readableDelta
20FROM KE
21ASOF LEFT JOIN CE ON (CE.Name = KE.Name) AND (CE.FirstTimestamp >= KE.FirstTimestamp)
22HAVING createTime > '1970-01-01 00:00:00'
23ORDER BY delta DESC
24LIMIT 5
```
