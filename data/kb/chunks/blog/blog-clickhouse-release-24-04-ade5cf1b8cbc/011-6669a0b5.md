For example, let’s say we initially wrote a query to find flights between San Francisco and New York, capturing both direct flights and ones that have a layover.

```
1SELECT
2    l.origin,
3    l.destination,
4    r.destination,
5    registration,
6    l.callsign,
7    r.callsign
8FROM opensky AS l
9LEFT JOIN opensky AS r ON l.destination = r.origin
10WHERE notEmpty(l.destination) 
11AND (l.origin = 'KSFO') 
12AND (r.destination = 'KJFK') 
13LIMIT 10
```

We later add an extra filter that only returns rows where `r.callsign = 'AAL1424'`.

```
1SELECT
2    l.origin,
3    l.destination AS leftDest,
4    r.destination AS rightDest,
5    registration AS reg,
6    l.callsign,
7    r.callsign
8FROM opensky AS l
9LEFT JOIN opensky AS r ON l.destination = r.origin
10WHERE notEmpty(l.destination) 
11AND (l.origin = 'KSFO') 
12AND (r.destination = 'KJFK') 
13AND (r.callsign = 'AAL1424')
14LIMIT 10
15SETTINGS optimize_move_to_prewhere = 0
```
