Below, we group by danceability, rounded to 1 decimal place, and plot the count. This gives us the distribution of the values.

```
1SELECT
2	round(danceability, 1) AS danceability,
3	bar(count(), 0, max(count()) OVER ()) AS dist
4FROM spotify
5GROUP BY danceability
6ORDER BY danceability ASC
```

Above we use the window function `max(count()) OVER ()` to determine the max count for each group, thus avoiding the need for us to specify a constant as the upper bound for the bar function.
