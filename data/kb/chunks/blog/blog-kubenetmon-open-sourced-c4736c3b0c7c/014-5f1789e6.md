## What we have been able to achieve \#

This is it when it comes to implementation! In some environments, we record up to a few million connection observations per minute, and while this sounds like a lot, ClickHouse compresses this data very well and can retrieve it in no time. And thanks to the TTL set in our table definition, the 90\-day retention is enforced automatically. Let’s take a look at how much data we store for just one of our regions:

```
1SELECT
2    formatReadableQuantity(sum(rows)),
3    formatReadableSize(sum(data_compressed_bytes)) AS compressed_size,
4    formatReadableSize(sum(data_uncompressed_bytes)) AS uncompressed_size,
5    sum(data_compressed_bytes) / sum(data_uncompressed_bytes) AS compression_ratio
6FROM system.parts
7WHERE (database = 'default') AND (`table` = 'network_flows_0') AND (active = 1);
```

Nearly 400 billion rows compressed from 86\.69 TiB down to just over 3% of the original size, or 2\.66 TiB. We can also check how individual columns compress:

```
1SELECT
2    name,
3    data_compressed_bytes / data_uncompressed_bytes AS compression_ratio,
4    formatReadableSize(data_uncompressed_bytes) AS uncompressed_size,
5    formatReadableSize(data_compressed_bytes) AS compressed_size
6FROM system.columns
7WHERE (database = 'default') AND (`table` = 'network_flows_0')
8ORDER BY compression_ratio ASC;
```
