Now, how can we use this table? For example, to look at the egress direction of connections opened by ClickHouse Server to external destinations, we would do something like this:

```
1SELECT intervalStartTime, sum(bytes)
2FROM loghouse.network_flows_0
3WHERE direction = 'out' AND localApp = 'clickhouse-server' AND connectionClass != 'INTRA_VPC'
4GROUP BY intervalStartTime
5ORDER BY intervalStartTime DESC
```
