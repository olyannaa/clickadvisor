# Announcing ClickHouse Observability \- fully managed observability on ClickHouse Cloud

![mike shi](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fmike_shi_5b7145e7d7.jpg&w=96&q=75)[Mike Shi](/authors/mike-shi)Nov 9, 2026 · 10 minutes readToday, we're introducing ClickHouse Observability in public beta: a fully managed observability solution for SREs and their agents, powered by ClickHouse Cloud and open\-source ClickStack.

Send your OpenTelemetry data to a managed OTLP endpoint, and we'll handle the rest. You get ClickHouse's query performance without having to size compute, tune your database and schemas, or manage ingestion.

Thanks to ClickHouse's efficiency, we can offer market\-leading pricing with a fully managed service and keep it simple: just gigabytes ingested for logs and traces ($0\.20/GB), and data points ingested for metrics ($0\.10/M).

### Try ClickHouse Observability, now in public beta. \#

Already using ClickHouse? **[Follow the getting started guide](https://clickhouse.com/docs/clickstack/observability/getting-started)** to turn on observability in your existing service.

[Sign up](https://console.clickhouse.cloud/signUp?intent=o11y&loc=blog-cta-2921-try-clickhouse-observability-now-in-public-beta-sign-up&glxid=eacb8509-de05-4687-9dca-cb40d67e476a&pagePath=%2Fblog%2Fannouncing-clickhouse-observability&origPath=%2Fblog%2Fsnowflake-clustering-vs-clickhouse-built-in-ordering&utm_ga=GA1.1.1957155534.1777812673)Get the benefits of ClickHouse for observability in minutes, with full\-fidelity logs, metrics, and traces ready to investigate. Explore your telemetry through the ClickStack UI or connect your own agents through purpose\-built MCP tools and SQL. Focus on understanding production and resolving failures while we operate the platform.

We're also announcing **ClickHouse Observability Apps in private preview**. While ClickHouse Observability makes the benefits of ClickHouse easier to access, Apps addresses a larger question: if agents can investigate and software can adapt to the task, why should observability interfaces stay fixed?
