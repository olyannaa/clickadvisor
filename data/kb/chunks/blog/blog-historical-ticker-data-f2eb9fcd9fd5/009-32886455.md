strayed from the advertised price \- when a large wave of selling overwhelms the available buyers, trades start executing at worse and worse prices, and slippage spikes. Once the selling eases and buyers return, it drops back down.

```
1SELECT
2    toStartOfSecond(t.ts) AS second, count() AS trades,
3    round(min(t.price), 2) AS low, round(max(t.price), 2) AS high,
4    round(sum(t.qty), 3) AS volume,
5    round(avg(abs(t.price - (q.bid_price + q.ask_price) / 2)), 4) AS avg_slippage
6FROM (
7    SELECT ts, price, qty, symbol
8    FROM trades
9    WHERE symbol = 'BTCUSDT'
10    AND ts >= '2024-01-16 14:44:00' AND ts < '2024-01-16 14:45:00'
11) AS t
12ASOF JOIN (
13    SELECT ts, bid_price, ask_price, symbol
14    FROM quotes
15    WHERE symbol = 'BTCUSDT'
16    AND ts >= '2024-01-16 14:44:00' AND ts < '2024-01-16 14:45:00'
17) AS q
18ON t.symbol = q.symbol AND t.ts >= q.ts
19GROUP BY second
20ORDER BY second;
```
