[![](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fboeing_vs_airbus_04895d9640.png&w=2048&q=75)](https://adsb.exposed/?zoom=3&lat=14.4347&lng=26.0156&query=ecd939dcdb623a87a0965dd7985c7646) Let's take a look at an SQL query for this visualization:

```
1WITH
2	bitShiftLeft(1::UInt64, {z:UInt8}) AS zoom_factor,
3	bitShiftLeft(1::UInt64, 32 - {z:UInt8}) AS tile_size,
4
5	tile_size * {x:UInt16} AS tile_x_begin,
6	tile_size * ({x:UInt16} + 1) AS tile_x_end,
7
8	tile_size * {y:UInt16} AS tile_y_begin,
9	tile_size * ({y:UInt16} + 1) AS tile_y_end,
10
11	mercator_x >= tile_x_begin AND mercator_x < tile_x_end
12	AND mercator_y >= tile_y_begin AND mercator_y < tile_y_end AS in_tile,
13
14	bitShiftRight(mercator_x - tile_x_begin, 32 - 10 - {z:UInt8}) AS x,
15	bitShiftRight(mercator_y - tile_y_begin, 32 - 10 - {z:UInt8}) AS y,
16
17	y * 1024 + x AS pos,
18
19	count() AS total,
20	sum(desc LIKE 'BOEING%') AS boeing,
21	sum(desc LIKE 'AIRBUS%') AS airbus,
22	sum(NOT (desc LIKE 'BOEING%' OR desc LIKE 'AIRBUS%')) AS other,
23
24	greatest(1000000 DIV {sampling:UInt32} DIV zoom_factor, total) AS max_total,
25	greatest(1000000 DIV {sampling:UInt32} DIV zoom_factor, boeing) AS max_boeing,
26	greatest(1000000 DIV {sampling:UInt32} DIV zoom_factor, airbus) AS max_airbus,
27	greatest(1000000 DIV {sampling:UInt32} DIV zoom_factor, other) AS max_other,
28
29	pow(total / max_total, 1/5) AS transparency,
30
31	255 * (1 + transparency) / 2 AS alpha,
32	pow(boeing, 1/5) * 256 DIV (1 + pow(max_boeing, 1/5)) AS red,
33	pow(airbus, 1/5) * 256 DIV (1 + pow(max_airbus, 1/5)) AS green,
34	pow(other, 1/5) * 256 DIV (1 + pow(max_other, 1/5)) AS blue
35
36SELECT round(red)::UInt8, round(green)::UInt8, round(blue)::UInt8, round(alpha)::UInt8
37FROM {table:Identifier}
38WHERE in_tile
39GROUP BY pos ORDER BY pos WITH FILL FROM 0 TO 1024*1024
```

The first part of the query calculates the condition `in_tile`, which is used in the `WHERE` section to filter the data in the requested tile. It then calculates the colors: alpha, red, green, and blue. They are adjusted by the [`pow`](https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/sql-reference/functions/math-functions#pow) function for better uniformity, clamped to the `0..255` range, and converted to `UInt8`. The sampling parameter is used for adjustment so that queries with a lower level of detail will return pictures with mostly the same relative colors. We group by the pixel coordinate pos and also use the [WITH FILL modifier](https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/sql-reference/statements/select/order-by#order-by-expr-with-fill-modifier) in the ORDER BY to fill zeros in the pixel positions that have no data. As a result, we will get an RGBA bitmap with the exact 1024x1024 size.
