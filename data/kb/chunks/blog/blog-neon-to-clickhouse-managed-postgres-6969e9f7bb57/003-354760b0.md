For the **stealth startup**, the issue was fit rather than failures: "Neon provided a great developer experience, but our production workloads require always\-on compute, logical replication, and predictable performance, so many of its serverless benefits didn't apply. Moving to always\-on Postgres reduced API latency from seconds to sub\-second and gave us a simpler path to OLAP and audit\-log workloads.

At the time, our Neon setup also lacked the straightforward FDW and OLAP\-extension integration we wanted. `pg_mooncake` required additional infrastructure we preferred not to manage, while `pg_clickhouse` was a major factor in our decision to evaluate ClickHouse Managed Postgres. Together, Postgres and the ClickHouse data warehouse now address most of our application and analytics needs, with our control plane and managed services handling the rest."

## What value have you seen since moving to ClickHouse Managed Postgres? \#

Every team described the improvement as immediate, and it came from the platform change alone rather than from rewriting queries or schema.

**Shipsidekick:** "The difference was immediate: zero downtime, a 99% cache hit rate, and peak workloads using only around 10% CPU on similarly sized compute. We are paying less today, with room to downsize even further."

**socialpruf.:** "The impact was immediate: zero connection dropouts or restarts, around 30% faster queries overall, and up to 5× faster performance for some queries. One key query dropped from 42 ms to 22 ms. We also eliminated network transfer costs and now reliably handle thousands of concurrent connections with PgBouncer."

**Infinitas Learning:** "With exactly the same schema, our p99 Postgres query latency improved from seconds to single\-digit milliseconds. We have also seen more reliable and predictable performance."

**Stealth startup:** "Customers immediately noticed faster APIs and application rendering, while our team spends less time working around query waterfalls with SSR and similar optimizations. Reliability was never our primary concern with Neon; the biggest gains have been consistently strong performance, greater control over infrastructure, and a direct path from Postgres to ClickHouse for analytics."
