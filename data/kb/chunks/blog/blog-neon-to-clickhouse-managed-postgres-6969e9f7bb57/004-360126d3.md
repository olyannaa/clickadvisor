Two things are worth pulling out here. First, these results are consistent with the benefits of ClickHouse Managed Postgres's architecture, which uses local NVMe storage to eliminate the network\-attached storage bottleneck. That helps deliver faster and, just as importantly, more predictable query performance. Second, for teams already using ClickHouse for analytics, both systems now live on the same platform, so the network transfer fees for streaming changes from Postgres to ClickHouse simply go away, and [pg\_clickhouse](https://clickhouse.com/docs/products/managed-postgres/extensions/pg_clickhouse/introduction) gives them a direct path from Postgres queries to the ClickHouse warehouse.

## How did you approach the migration and what was the overall experience? \#

This was the part we were most curious about, because migration risk is usually what keeps teams on a platform they've outgrown. Three of the four used [ClickPipes](https://clickhouse.com/docs/products/managed-postgres/migrations/clickpipes), which is built into ClickHouse Managed Postgres and handles schema migration, an optimized initial load using parallel snapshotting, and change data capture (CDC) to keep both databases in sync until cutover. The fourth, with a multitenant data plane spread across many Neon projects, built its own migration service on top of native Postgres tooling.

> "Our 750 GB database was migrated in under 16 hours. In comparison, migrating a smaller 250 GB database to Neon took three days and required significant manual intervention."
> 
> 
> — Jerome van den Heuvel, Engineering Manager, Infinitas Learning
