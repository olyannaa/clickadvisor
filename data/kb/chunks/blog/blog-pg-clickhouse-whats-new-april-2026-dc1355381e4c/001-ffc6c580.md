# What's New in pg\_clickhouse \- JSONB Support, SQL value functions, Streaming, and more

![image 512x512 1](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FImage_512x512_1_8bc569c360.png&w=96&q=75)[David Wheeler](/authors/david-wheeler)Apr 24, 2026 · 13 minutes readWe've been gratified by the community reception of [pg\_clickhouse](https://github.com/ClickHouse/pg_clickhouse "pg_clickhouse on GitHub"), the extension to query ClickHouse databases from Postgres. Recent uptake generated a ton of feedback, which we've been diligently addressing in the last few releases. These changes follow our constant mantra for pg\_clickhouse: pushdown, pushdown, pushdown! Let's take a quick tour.
