This dataset warrants an entire blog on possible queries. ### Ether supply by day \#

The original BigQuery query, documented as part of [Awesome BigQuery views](https://github.com/blockchain-etl/awesome-bigquery-views/blob/master/ethereum/ether-supply-by-day.sql) and discussed [here](https://medium.com/google-cloud/how-to-query-ether-supply-in-bigquery-90f8ae795a8), executes in 6 seconds. The optimized ClickHouse query runs in 0\.009s, a big difference when comparing ClickHouse to BigQuery.

![ether_supply.png](/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fether_supply_26efdd3501.png&w=2048&q=75)

```
1ALTER TABLE traces ADD PROJECTION trace_type_projection (
2                    SELECT trace_type,
3                    toStartOfDay(block_timestamp) as date, sum(value) as value GROUP BY trace_type, date
4                    )
5ALTER TABLE traces MATERIALIZE PROJECTION trace_type_projection
6
7WITH ether_emitted_by_date AS
8    (
9        SELECT
10            date,
11            sum(value) AS value
12        FROM traces
13        WHERE trace_type IN ('genesis', 'reward')
14        GROUP BY toStartOfDay(block_timestamp) AS date
15    )
16SELECT
17    date,
18    sum(value) OVER (ORDER BY date ASC) / power(10, 18) AS supply
19FROM ether_emitted_by_date
