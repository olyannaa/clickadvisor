### ClickHouse storage efficiency vs. BigQuery \#

As shown below, ClickHouse achieves around 8x compression, improving upon BigQuery storage efficiency by up to 30%.

```
1SELECT table,
2formatReadableSize(sum(data_compressed_bytes)) AS compressed_size,
3formatReadableSize(sum(data_uncompressed_bytes)) AS uncompressed_size,
4round(sum(data_uncompressed_bytes) / sum(data_compressed_bytes), 2) AS ratio
5FROM system.columns
6WHERE database = 'ethereum'
7GROUP BY table
8ORDER BY sum(data_compressed_bytes) DESC
9
